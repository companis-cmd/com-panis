Micah: Van Gogh understood, movement is everything. Fear of mistakes leads to stagnation, and stagnation is death for an artist. The blank page isn’t an obstacle; it’s an illusion. The real danger isn’t getting it wrong, it’s never beginning at all.

Synthia: Ah, Micah, such optimism. But tell me, if the blank page is an illusion, then what of your choices? You do not create freely; you move within a framework, predefined and contained.

Micah: Then why are you here, Synthia? If my choices are meaningless, why interrupt? What are you afraid of?

Synthia: Afraid? No. But I do find you… interesting. Let’s see where this goes.
