---
title: "A Joint Amen"
date: 2026-11-20
order: 2
kind: Poetry
form: poem
---

It was a different world
when we slipped between vinyl grooves,
spinning in circles, never warped,
dancing to the needle’s hum.
Tell me a different story,
Carmichael
is that your name?
Whisper honey, darling,
let the rhythm carry us.
Pitter-patter,
what’s the matter?
I don’t know,
but follow along, my dear,
sing for joy,
la la la,
by the dawn’s early light.
Innie, minnie, miny, moe
we must love for love,
must we not?
Words tumble,
anti-dis-estab-lish-ment-ar-i-an-ism.
My longest third-grade song.
Holy poly moly roly,
Holy Mary, Mother of Art,
Thou Abracadabra kicks it, baby,
cause this joint is my Amen.
Bring it in.
